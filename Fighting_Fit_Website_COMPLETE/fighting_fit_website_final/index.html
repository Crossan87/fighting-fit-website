ABOUT FIGHTING FIT

REAL COACHING. NO NONSENSE.

Fighting Fit PT & Boxing combines personal training with boxing-based coaching. Sessions are built around the individual and can focus on strength, fitness, weight management, boxing technique, conditioning or a mix of all of them.

Beginners are welcome. You do not need to be a fighter to train like one.

SELF DEFENCE CLASSES

AWARENESS. CONFIDENCE. PRACTICAL SKILLS.

Self defence classes focus on personal safety, awareness, confident boundaries and simple practical skills that can help create space and get to safety.

  • Situational awareness and personal safety
  • Verbal boundaries and confident responses
  • Simple escape and striking fundamentals
  • Controlled scenario-based learning
